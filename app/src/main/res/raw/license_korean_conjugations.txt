Korean conjugation data
=======================

The generated Korean conjugation forms in core/data/ko_conj.tsv were produced
at build time with KoParadigm and Kiwi. They are distributed as part of the
Type1 Korean dictionary under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), together with
the other Type1 dictionary data.

KoParadigm
----------
KoParadigm: Korean Inflectional Paradigm Generator
https://github.com/Kyubyong/KoParadigm
Copyright Kyubyong Park and contributors
Licence: Apache License 2.0
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

KoParadigm's verb and ending inventory is derived from the Standard Korean
Language Dictionary (표준국어대사전), published by the National Institute of
Korean Language, which is distributed under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR.
https://stdict.korean.go.kr/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/kr/

Kiwi
----
Kiwi: Korean Intelligent Word Identifier
https://github.com/bab2min/Kiwi
Copyright Minchul Lee and contributors
Licence: Apache License 2.0
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The Kiwi model files used at build time are also Apache-2.0. They were trained
on the 21st Century Sejong Corpus and the National Institute of Korean Language
"모두의 말뭉치" corpora. The source corpora are not redistributed with Type1.

The generated conjugations are derived data. Attribution is given here and in
core/data/NOTICE.md; redistributed derived data remains under the same
share-alike terms.
