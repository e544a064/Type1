Derived data used by Type1: every single alphabetic token up to 31 characters
with a positive wordfreq probability (about 287,000 forms), including names,
places, brands and modern terms. Common American spellings are replaced by
their British variants, while words valid in both varieties are kept. The
pronoun "I" is kept capitalised.

The derived data is distributed with the GPL-3.0 application under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for the data itself.
