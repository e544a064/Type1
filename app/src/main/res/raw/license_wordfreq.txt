Derived data used by Type1: every single English alphabetic token up to 31
characters (about 287,000 forms), every single Spanish token up to 31 characters
(about 334,000 forms), and every single Korean token up to 31 precomposed Hangul
syllables (about 26,800 forms), all with a positive wordfreq probability. The
English data includes names, places, brands and modern terms; common American
spellings are replaced by their British variants, while words valid in both
varieties are kept. The pronoun "I" is kept capitalised.

The derived data is distributed with the GPL-3.0 application under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for the data itself.
