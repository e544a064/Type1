Derived data used by Type1: lowercase single tokens made of letters and inner
apostrophes, with film and book frequencies summed per orthographic form.
Spaces, hyphens and other non-token punctuation are excluded because the
keyboard tokenizer cannot use them. All remaining forms with positive frequency
and at most 31 characters are kept (about 121,000 entries).

The derived data is distributed with the GPL-3.0 application under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 terms for the data itself.
